Who we are

Storytellers behind the camera and the control desk

Established in 2017, IOKI has grown into a production house with a team of about six dedicated members who share a passion for storytelling and event production. Over the years, we have traveled across various districts of Nagaland as well as to other states in Northeast India, bringing our expertise to a wide range of events.

With experience in more than 200 projects, our work spans from intimate conferences and funerals to weddings, large festivals, and live broadcasts. Each project has reinforced our commitment to delivering high-quality, reliable, and creative solutions tailored to every client's needs.
