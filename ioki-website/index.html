Who we are

Est. 2017 · Nagaland

IOKI is a production house of about six people who share a passion for storytelling and event production.

Since 2017 we have traveled across the districts of Nagaland and to other states in Northeast India, bringing our crew and equipment to a wide range of events.

Over 200 projects so far, from intimate conferences and funerals to weddings, large festivals and live broadcasts. Every job is planned around what the client needs, and delivered reliably.
