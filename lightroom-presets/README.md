# Rajwada Nights – Lightroom presets

| Preset | Look |
|---|---|
| `Rajwada_Bright.xmp` | Look of photo 1: bright, clean, open shadows, vivid reds, golden-lime greens, natural skin |
| `Rajwada_Moody_Gold.xmp` | Look of photo 2: darker and moodier, amber/golden glow, deep reds, olive-gold greens, soft vignette |

**Import in Lightroom mobile:** download `Rajwada_Presets.zip` (or the .xmp files) to your phone, open any photo → **Presets** → **⋮** → **Import Presets** → pick the file. They show up in the group **Rajwada Nights**.

If your photo is too bright or too dark after applying, adjust **Exposure** only. The colours stay the same.
